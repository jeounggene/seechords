What you get

Synced chord strip

Scrolling chord blocks locked to playback—see what to play and when.

Diagrams

SVG guitar chord diagrams for previous, current, and next chords.

Transpose & timing

Shift chords by semitones and nudge sync if the video is slightly off.

Community data

When chords exist for a video, they load instantly. You can contribute by uploading matching audio for analysis—audio is processed and deleted; only chord data is kept.
